# Prototype stakeholder validation

The seed includes five clearly labeled simulated role-persona responses. They are fictional prompts for demonstrating the feedback workflow, not stakeholder participation or research results. The app also includes a feedback form with role, tested task, 1–5 rating, comments and observed issue. Any records entered during a demonstration are prototype evaluation feedback. No real stakeholder participation is claimed.

Suggested usability tasks: sign in and acknowledge notice; locate SYN-0007; inspect an unresolved action; run shift transfer; acknowledge receipt; resolve an assigned item; find its audit trail; inspect experiment comparison.

Ask participants to rate ease of use, clarity, unresolved risk visibility, ownership clarity, shift-change usefulness, information overload and trust (1–5), plus open feedback. Recruit across nursing, coordination, surgery, anaesthesia and administration only under appropriate research governance. Current simulated data cannot establish clinical usability or safety.
